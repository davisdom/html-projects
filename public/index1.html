The Best Movies According to Dom

My top 3 movies of all-time

Major Pain

A hilarious, old-school military comedy featuring Major Payne's outrageous discipline, unforgettable personality, and unconventional training methods.

Longest Yard

A classic sports comedy where prison inmates face off against the guards in an intense football game. Featuring unforgettable characters, including Cheeseburger Eddie!

The Incredible Hulk

An action-packed superhero movie featuring Bruce Banner and his incredible transformation into the Hulk. Two words: HULK SMASH!
